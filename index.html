01 · our story

Somehow,
it became us.

At first, we were just two people who didn’t know each other. I never really noticed you until the moment you annoyed me when I asked you to play the GSCPW music. It seemed like such a small moment, but somehow, it became the beginning of something I never expected. Then came Soe, followed by those two weeks of practice, filled with little conversations, laughter, and moments that slowly brought us closer.

Then we started with messages, calls, teasing, random conversations, and an unreasonable amount of “yapping” slowly became something much more. Somewhere between the jokes and the serious talks, we started choosing each other.

And maybe that is what makes this special: it was not one dramatic moment. It was a collection of tiny moments that kept adding up.

“What means the most to me is when you think of me without forcing yourself to.”

08 · a letter for you

Sayangku Naila,

I don't think I can put everything I feel into one page. But if I had to choose one thing to say, it would be thank you.

Thank you for letting me know the little things about you. Thank you for telling me how you want to be loved instead of asking me to somehow magically know. Thank you for the late-night talks, the random jokes, the stubborn moments, the soft moments, the overwhelming moments, and all the tiny pieces in between.

I hope you never feel like you have to become “easy to love”. You do not have to hide your feelings, translate every emotion perfectly, or pretend everything is fine just so you won't be a burden.

I want to keep learning you — slowly, honestly, and intentionally. Not just the obvious things, but the small things that everyone else might miss.

Because if loving you is partly about noticing, then I want to keep noticing.
